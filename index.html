Interpretation

The supervised Land Use/Land Cover classification of Hyderabad using Sentinel-2 imagery and the Random Forest classifier indicates that Built-up land is the dominant land-cover class, occupying approximately 66.86% of the total study area. Cropland is the second largest class, covering 24.14%, while Bare/Open Land accounts for 5.16%. Water bodies and Vegetation occupy relatively small proportions of 2.75% and 1.62%, respectively. The overall classification accuracy of 76.36% and a Kappa coefficient of 0.705 indicate a reasonably reliable classification. Some confusion was observed between Built-up and Bare/Open Land because of similar spectral characteristics in Sentinel-2 imagery. The Random Forest classifier effectively handled multiple spectral bands and produced stable classification results.
